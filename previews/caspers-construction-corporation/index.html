Meet the Builder

Built on One Man's Commitment to Quality Since 1968

In 1968, Ed Caspers started this company with a truck, a set of tools, and a simple belief: build every home like it's your own, and treat every family like a neighbor.

More than five decades later, that hasn't changed. Ed has personally guided homes across Long Beach, from first kitchen remodels to ground-up custom builds. He still walks the job sites, still answers the phone, and still measures a job the same way he always has, by the handshake at the end and the families who call again years later.

For Ed, this was never about building houses. It's about building a name his family and his community can trust, one project at a time.
