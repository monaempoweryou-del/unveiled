Fair questions

Before you book

Is this just a sales call?

No. It's a working session focused on your business. If a done-for-you build makes sense afterward we'll tell you, but the goal is to help you make better decisions, not to pitch you.

What's the difference between the two options?

The AI Strategy Consultation is with an implementation specialist and is ideal for mapping opportunities, tools, and a roadmap. "Consult with Moe" is a premium private session directly with Maor for deeper strategy, systems, and second opinions.

What do I get out of it?

A clear action plan for your business, decisions made with expert input, and a prioritized view of where AI pays off, so you can move without the expensive trial and error.

How does scheduling work?

Pick a time on the page, and we confirm it with a Zoom link and a short intake. Sessions are one-on-one and remote.

What if I then want you to build it?

Perfect. The plan you leave with maps directly to what we can implement for you. A consultation is the natural step before a full implementation.
