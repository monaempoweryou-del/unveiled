The five steps

1

Pick the one message that wins you work

Not ten. One. The single thing a customer needs to hear to trust you. Write it as you would say it out loud, in plain words. That sentence is the spine of your first video.

2

Capture one clean photo and 30 seconds of your voice

A well lit photo facing the camera, and a short clip of you talking so the avatar sounds like you and not a robot. That is the whole raw material. No studio, no crew.

3

Write the script with the prompt below

Paste the prompt into your AI tool, drop in your one message, and it hands back a 30 to 45 second script built to hook, help, and invite. Ready to speak, no editing needed.

4

Generate the avatar video

Feed your photo, your voice, and the script into an avatar generator. In a few minutes you have a vertical video of you delivering the message, clean and on brand.

5

Post it, then do it again tomorrow

One message becomes one video becomes one post. Repeat with the next message. Five in a row and you have a week of presence built in an afternoon.

The prompt

Copy this, replace the two brackets, and paste it into your AI tool.

Avatar Script Prompt
You are my short-form video scriptwriter. Write a 30 to 45 second spoken script for a vertical AI avatar video. My business: [WHAT YOU DO, FOR WHOM] The one message: [THE SINGLE THING A CUSTOMER NEEDS TO HEAR TO TRUST YOU] Rules: - Open with a hook in the first 3 seconds that names the viewer's real problem. - Speak like a person, not a brochure. Short sentences. No jargon. - Lead with the outcome the customer gets, not the tools behind it. - End with one clear next step to work with me. - No hashtags inside the script. No stage directions. Just the words I say. Give me the script only, ready to read aloud.
