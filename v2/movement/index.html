The manifesto

Something extraordinary is happening. For the first time, humanity isn't just using a new technology. We're helping shape it.

Every day, millions of people open an AI and ask, solve, build, write, create, learn, and teach. Most believe they're simply using a tool. But every interaction adds to something far larger, a collective effort, billions of hours, teaching these systems to think and work more like us.

And AI is creating extraordinary value. Industries are being remade. Historic companies are being born. This isn't something to fear. It's something to help shape.

If humanity is helping build the future of AI, humanity deserves a meaningful voice in that future.

Not to slow progress. Not to stop it. To make sure it includes the people helping create it. We are not against AI, we build with it every day, and we believe in it. We simply believe the human contribution deserves recognition, representation, and a seat at the table as this technology remakes the world.

If you believe humanity deserves a voice in the future it's helping create, add yours.

Fair questions

The questions people ask first.

Is this anti-AI?

No, the opposite. We build and implement AI every day and believe it will improve life in countless ways. We just believe humanity should stay part of the conversation as it reshapes the world. Pro-human is not anti-AI.

Is this a lawsuit?

No. This is a public conversation and an advocacy initiative, not a legal claim. We're not asking anyone to sue anyone or promising anyone a payout. Our goal is awareness, participation, and a credible platform that could inform future public and policy discussion. If serious policy or legal ideas emerge later, they'll be pursued transparently and with proper counsel, never as a promise made here.

Are you saying people are owed money?

No. This isn't about back-pay or a check. It's about recognition, representation, and a seat at the table. The principle is simple: a technology built on humanity's collective knowledge should keep humanity in the conversation about its future.

Why should I support this?

Because decisions being made now, by a small number of players, will shape work, wealth, and daily life for everyone. Adding your voice helps show that ordinary people want to be part of that conversation, not just live with the outcome.

Why now?

Because the direction of AI is being set right now, while it's still early. The conversation about who shapes it, and who shares in it, is far easier to influence today than after the outcomes are locked in.

What happens after I add my voice?

You join a growing community of supporters. We'll keep you updated with the manifesto, new episodes, research we publish, and milestones as the movement grows. No spam, and you can leave anytime.

Who's behind this?

It's an initiative started by Maor and the team at UNVEILED, an AI-implementation company. We build AI for a living, which is exactly why we care how its future is shaped. We'll always be transparent about who we are and how this is run.
