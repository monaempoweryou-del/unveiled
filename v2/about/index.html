The Person Behind the Company

I'm Maor. I implement AI the way it should have been done from the start.

Not a tech company that discovered marketing. A marketer who spent years inside real businesses, then discovered what AI actually changes when you implement it correctly.

Where this started

Before UNVEILED there was the agency. Through MONA Digital Marketing I have spent years working hands-on with the businesses nobody builds software for: builders, locksmiths, contractors, drivers, salons, family companies. Google Ads, SEO, websites, reviews, the whole unglamorous machine that keeps a local business fed.

That work teaches you something no AI demo ever will: business owners do not want technology. They want the phone to ring, the schedule to fill, and their evenings back.

Why I went all in on AI

When AI arrived, I did what every owner I know did: I tried the tools. Dozens of them. And I watched the same thing happen to me that happens to my clients: hours burned learning interfaces, prompts that worked once, subscriptions nobody cancels, and no actual change in the business.

The tools were never the problem. The missing piece was implementation: someone who builds the system into your business until it runs without you thinking about it.

So I stopped collecting tools and started implementing. First inside my own companies, where every experiment costs me, not a client. What survived became the systems we now build for others.

The avatar you keep seeing

The bald guy with the beard in our videos? That is my AI twin. I built him because I was the bottleneck in my own content: I knew exactly what to say and never had the hours to film it.

Now he shows up every day: my face, my voice, my message, while the real me is with clients. He is not a gimmick. He is proof, running in public, that this works. Everything we sell, we run on ourselves first.

Why UNVEILED exists

UNVEILED is the company I wished existed when I watched smart owners waste months on tools: a team of experts who implement AI correctly the first time, take responsibility for the outcome, and stay until it runs.

We do not sell AI. Nobody wants AI to run their business. They want it to improve it: more revenue, more productivity, fewer wasted hours. That is the only measure we care about.

Where this is going

The vision is bigger than any single service. We are building an AI implementation ecosystem for business owners: the roadmap that shows you where AI pays off, the systems that run your marketing and operations, the avatar that keeps your face in front of customers, and the team that stands behind all of it.

You describe the outcome. The system finds the execution. That is the standard we hold ourselves to, and the future we are building toward, one correctly implemented business at a time.
